# ChartVolt — 5-Minute Intro Pitch

**Audience:** Prospective users / clients seeing the platform for the first time.
**Target runtime:** 5:00 (≈ 720 spoken words at a conversational pitch pace).
**Tone:** Energetic, modern, confident. Think a sports-tech / fintech product launch.
**Music:** Upbeat electronic underscore at ~110 BPM; drops on each section change, swells on the CTA.

> Stage directions are in **[brackets]**. Lower-thirds / on-screen text are in `monospace`. Voiceover lines are in plain prose.

---

## SCENE 1 — Cold Open (0:00 → 0:15)

**[Visual]** Tight macro shot of a candlestick chart racing upward. Quick cuts: a stopwatch starting, a finger swiping a card, a confetti pop on a winner's profile, a leaderboard rank flipping from #14 to #3.

**[Sound]** Subtle whoosh into a synth pulse.

**Voiceover:**

> "Trading shouldn't feel like a spreadsheet. It should feel like a sport."

**[On-screen text]** `Welcome to ChartVolt.` *(fades in as the music drops)*

---

## SCENE 2 — The Pitch (0:15 → 0:55)

**[Visual]** ChartVolt logo settles centre-screen, then dissolves into a hero shot of the dashboard — Player Profile card, leaderboard rank, equity chart, ongoing competition tile.

**Voiceover:**

> "ChartVolt is a competitive, gamified trading platform. You trade live forex markets with practice capital — no real money at risk — and you compete head-to-head against other traders for prizes, ranking points, and bragging rights.
>
> Every trade you place, every position you close, every win — they all count toward your global score, your level, your badges, and your reputation on the leaderboard.
>
> It's everything you love about trading, with none of the financial risk… and all the dopamine of a competitive game."

**[On-screen text]** `Practice trading. Real markets. Real competition.`

---

## SCENE 3 — Trade Like a Pro, or Play Like a Gamer (0:55 → 1:35)

**[Visual]** Split-screen: left side shows **Pro Mode** — full order form with stop-loss, take-profit, leverage slider, candlestick chart with indicators. Right side shows **Easy Mode** — large green and red buttons, "Higher" / "Lower" style controls, simplified P&L display.

**Voiceover:**

> "Choose your style. **Pro Mode** gives you full control — leverage up to 100x, stop-loss, take-profit, multiple chart timeframes, technical indicators, and live spreads updated by our real-time price engine.
>
> Prefer something simpler? **Easy Mode** strips it down to the essentials, so anyone can place a trade in seconds — perfect if you're new to the markets.
>
> Either way, the prices are live, the candles are real, and your P&L updates in real time — second by second."

**[On-screen text]** `Pro Mode  |  Easy Mode  |  Real-time prices`

## SCENE 4 — Competitions: Where the Money Is (1:35 → 2:20)

**[Visual]** Competition lobby tile flips open. Show: prize pool counter, countdown timer, leaderboard ticking live with multiple participants, a winner's confetti burst.

**Voiceover:**

> "Every day, new competitions open across every prize tier you can imagine. Pay your entry, get a starting balance, and trade for a fixed window — minutes, hours, or days.
>
> When the timer hits zero, the rankings lock in. Top finishers split the prize pool. The Game Master who created the competition takes a small organizer cut. The platform takes a small fee. And you walk away with credits you can keep, spend, or cash out.
>
> Different competitions have different ranking methods — pure profit, ROI percentage, win rate — and different rules for leverage, drawdown, and trade limits. Pick the format that suits your style."

**[On-screen text]** `Open the lobby. Pay entry. Climb the ranks. Get paid.`

## Production Notes

- **Word count:** ~720 spoken words. Reading at a moderately fast pitch pace (≈ 150 wpm) lands at 4:48; the cold-open beat, scene transitions, and CTA hold bring it to 5:00.
- **Captions:** Burn in clean, modern sans-serif captions throughout. ~60 % of social audiences watch with sound off.
- **B-roll priorities (record these first):**
  1. Live dashboard with real candle motion (10-sec clip per scene)
  2. Competition leaderboard ticking
  3. Match Card swipe interaction
  4. VS screen animation
  5. Journey map traverse with milestone pop
  6. Badge / level-up celebration
  7. Marketplace browsing scroll
  8. Game Master earnings counter
- **Variants to also produce from this script:**
  - **0:60 trailer** — Scenes 1, 4, 5, 11 only.
  - **0:30 social cut** — Scenes 1, 5, 11 only.
  - **2:00 investor pitch** — Same beats; replace "Sign up today" CTA with "Talk to our team."
- **Localisation:** All on-screen text is grouped at scene level so a translator can swap them without re-editing footage.
- **Compliance note:** The script frames ChartVolt as practice / simulated trading with no real-money risk. If any region requires explicit disclosure, add an end-card: `"All trading on ChartVolt is simulated. No live market exposure."`
